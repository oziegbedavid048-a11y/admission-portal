# Brag Plan: Apply Gabstep

## What is this app?
Apply Gabstep (apply.gabstep.com) is a study-abroad application portal: an applicant
signs up, picks a course country by country, uploads documents, pays the fee in their
own currency and follows every stage to the offer letter and visa; partner agents file
students and earn with each one they place.

## The angle
A premium, vertical launch film that plays the real journey end to end in about twenty
seconds: from a crowd of graduates throwing caps, to one student's actual path through
the portal (a real Paris course, the three real documents, the five real stages), landing
on the moment the app itself celebrates: the offer letter, with its multicolour confetti.
The claim is the site's own: "Begin your global education journey."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Full-bleed graduates photo (the site's own hero image), toned deep green, pushing in.
"Begin your global" / "education journey" slams up line by line on the first beats.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A country is chosen (France), the real school card lands (Paris Business Academy (PBA),
  Paris, France) and the real course card under it (Bachelor's in Web Marketing, 3 Years,
  €7,500/yr, intake September / October & January). A cursor taps Apply.
- Passport, Transcripts and CV tick off one by one; then "Pay the fee in your own currency".
- The five real stages light up in order; at "Offer letter decision" the app's
  "Offer of admission / Congratulations" card pops with confetti.

## Outro / punchline
Brand lockup: the two-leaf mark + "Apply Gabstep", the line "Apply in four simple steps.",
a "Start your application" pill and apply.gabstep.com. A quiet agent line beneath:
"Agents: earn with every student you place."

## User flow worth showing
Pick a course (country → school → course → Apply) → upload documents and pay the fee in
your own currency → track every stage to the offer letter (celebration).

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium study-abroad launch film, vertical, with cinematic touches
- Interpretation: few scenes with confident holds, fast entrances (0.3-0.6s) then settle;
  restraint in colour (deep greens, cream product UI) with one multicolour payoff.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 21.5s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #04231b (--g-deep) with #0a3a2c (--g-deep-2) depth
- Accent: #0b5c43 (--g-accent) for actions; #7fbe9c / #a9c6bb (--g-chart-1 / --g-on-deep-2) on dark
- Product surfaces: #ffffff cards on #fbf7ef (--g-canvas), ink #08211b (--g-ink), #54665f (--g-ink-2)
- Leaf mark: #65e005 / #107b00 (traced from the original logo)
- Display / body font: the site uses the system stack (SF Pro / Inter); the video uses
  Montserrat (bundled, geometric, close to the site's bold headings)
- Strongest visual element: the hero graduates photo; the admission celebration confetti

## Share copy (draft)
Apply Gabstep: begin your global education journey. Pick a course, apply, pay the fee in
your own currency and follow every stage to your offer letter, at apply.gabstep.com.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (steady and clean, ~110 BPM)
- Music treatment: in from 0s at ~0.32, fade out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset `assets/music/cues/...vol-12...music-cues.json`; strong cues
  17.47s (offer letter payoff) and 18.56s (outro lockup); beat grid ~0.55s apart, so text
  reveals use every other beat or reveal-then-hold
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the background glow breathes with the bed's energy
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: hook slam, cursor taps, document ticks (first and last), offer
  letter bell, outro lockup
- Restraint rule: nothing harsh, no stacked hits, SFX under the music

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 0.0–2.9s
Graduates photo full-bleed, deep-green tone, slow push-in. Lines "Begin your global" /
"education journey" (verbatim hero copy) rise in one after the other and hold.
Sequential/interaction: yes, two lines in sequence.
Audio intent: confident open. Audio-coupled idea: soft impact on the first line.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2 (photo dims and drops back, content swaps).

### Scene 2 — Reveal — 2.9–5.4s
Two-leaf mark draws in, "Apply Gabstep" settles beside it, then "Fifteen years of opening
doors abroad." (verbatim landing copy) and "apply.gabstep.com".
Sequential/interaction: mark, then name, then line.
Audio intent: arrival. Audio-coupled idea: soft drop on the lockup.
Transition mood: slide → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Pick a course — 5.4–9.8s
Label "1 · Pick a course". Chips France / Spain; France is tapped. School card lands:
Paris Business Academy (PBA), Paris, France. Course card: Bachelor's in Web Marketing,
3 Years, €7,500/yr, Intake September / October & January. Cursor taps "Apply".
Sequential/interaction: yes, simulated taps on the chip and Apply.
Audio-coupled idea: click on each tap.
Transition mood: slide → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Upload and pay — 9.8–13.6s
Label "2 · Upload and pay". Rows Passport, Transcripts, CV each tick done, then the line
"Pay the fee in your own currency." with a "Pay securely" button that is tapped.
Sequential/interaction: three ticks then a tap; reveal fast, hold all.
Audio-coupled idea: drop on first and last tick, click on pay.
Transition mood: slide → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Track to the offer — 13.6–18.3s
Label "3 · Track every stage". The five real stages light up in order with a filling
line: Submitted & payment confirmed, Document verification, Institution review, Offer
letter decision, Visa guidance & enrolment. At ~17.47s (beat-locked) the app's
celebration card pops: "Offer of admission" / "Congratulations, Ada" / "Paris Business
Academy (PBA) has offered you a place." with multicolour confetti (the app's own colours).
Ada is a fictional stand-in, not a real applicant.
Audio-coupled idea: bell on the celebration.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 6.

### Scene 6 — Outro — 18.3–21.5s
Lockup lands on ~18.56s (beat-locked): mark + Apply Gabstep, "Apply in four simple
steps.", pill "Start your application", "apply.gabstep.com", and the small agent line.
Audio intent: resolve; music fades out.

**Music mood for this video:** warm, confident, cinematic-polished
**Audio summary:** a clean steady bed carries the journey, with a few soft taps and one
bright bell on the offer letter, fading out under the final lockup.
