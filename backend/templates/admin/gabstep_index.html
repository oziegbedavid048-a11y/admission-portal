Where each job is done
  1. Documents: open an upload, look at it, then Verify or Reject with a comment. The comment is emailed to the applicant. When every document on a file is verified, the file's documents check ticks itself.
  2. Applications: students who applied themselves. Verify personal details, academic details and the fee; send to the university; grant admission or decline; email a message. One action, one job.
  3. Agent applications: every partner agent. Click an agent to see the students they registered. Download on a student gives one ZIP with their details and every document.
  4. Payments to confirm (Payments): bank transfers with a receipt. Check the money arrived, then Confirm payment, which credits the agent's commission, or Reject with a reason.
  5. Letters: add a letter to an application, then Publish it. The applicant is emailed and sees it in their dashboard.
  6. Visa Support Queue: admitted students. Start visa processing, then confirm the visa.
  7. Correction requests: approve or decline a requested change.
  8. Support messages (Accounts): open one, type a reply and save. The reply is emailed.
  9. Partners & sales and Add course: partner payouts and the course catalogue.
