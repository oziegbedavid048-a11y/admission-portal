Where each job is done
  1. Documents: open an upload, look at it, then Verify or Reject with a comment. The comment is emailed to the applicant. When every document on a file is verified, the file's documents check ticks itself.
  2. Applications: verify personal details, academic details and the fee; send to the university; grant admission or decline; email a message. One action, one job.
  3. Letters: add a letter to an application, then Publish it. The applicant is emailed and sees it in their dashboard.
  4. Visa Support Queue: admitted students. Start visa processing, then confirm the visa.
  5. Correction requests: approve or decline a requested change.
  6. Support messages (Accounts): open one, type a reply and save. The reply is emailed.
  7. Payments, Partners & sales and Add course: fees paid off platform, partner payouts, and the course catalogue.
